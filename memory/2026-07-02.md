## Key Architecture Decisions
- Express 5 uses `path-to-regexp` v8+ → routes use named params: `/{*path}` not `*`
- yahoo-finance2 `options()` expects `date` in `YYYY-MM-DD` format, NOT epoch
- yahoo-finance2 **mutates** the `dateOption` object — `date: "2026-08-21"` becomes `date: "2026-08-21T00:00:00.000Z"` after calling `yf.options()`. Must capture `cleanedDate` BEFORE the call.
- Outside market hours: `greeks: undefined`, `bid: 0`, `ask: 0`, `impliedVolatility: 0.00001`
- Frontend expects IV as decimal (multiplies by 100 for display)
