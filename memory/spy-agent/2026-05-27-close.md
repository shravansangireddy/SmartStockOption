## 🧠 Lessons Learned

### Lesson #1: VIX Divergence Doesn't Always Mean Danger
- **What happened:** We flagged VIX divergence (VIX rising while SPY rising) as a risk. Today, VIX dropped -3.0% to 16.50 — the lowest in recent days.
- **Lesson:** VIX at these levels (16-17) is already in "complacency" territory. Further drops just confirm the bull, not signal a top. The divergence signal we were tracking resolved in the bull's favor.
- **Adjustment:** Lower the VIX concern threshold. VIX divergence is more relevant when VIX is >20. At sub-17, the signal is just "low volatility" not "impending crash."

### Lesson #2: Pullback Targets Need to Be Dynamic
- **What happened:** Morning pullback target was $740-745. Actual pullback was only to ~$749.62. We adjusted midday to $747-750, which was closer.
- **Lesson:** In a strong trend near highs, pullbacks are often shallow (1-2% max). Setting pullback targets too deep means you never get filled.
- **Adjustment:** Use percentage-based pullback targets: near 52W highs, expect 0.5-1.5% pullbacks, not 2-3%. Calculate as: `Entry Zone = Current Price × (1 - 0.005 to 0.015)`

### Lesson #3: Flat Days at Resistance Are Actually Bullish
- **What happened:** SPY closed essentially flat (-0.03%) after failing a breakout. Normally, failed breakouts lead to selling. Instead, SPY held and recovered to close near the open.
- **Lesson:** When price holds near resistance without selling off, buyers are absorbing supply. This is accumulation, not distribution. The next attempt at $752 may succeed.
- **Adjustment:** Add "hold at resistance" as a bullish signal — flat days at highs with no volume spike on selling = accumulation.

### Lesson #4: MACD Is Currently Useless for SPY
- **What happened:** 3-month backtest shows 0 MACD trades. The last signal was exited May 18 and MACD has been silent since.
- **Lesson:** In trending markets near highs, MACD crossover strategies go dormant after the trend is established. The signal that got you in is gone, and the signal to get back in hasn't triggered.
- **Adjustment:** Supplement MACD with a trend-following overlay. When MACD is flat and price is above the 50-day EMA, maintain a bullish bias regardless of MACD signal. Don't wait for MACD to re-trigger — it may not until a pullback deep enough to cross the signal line.
