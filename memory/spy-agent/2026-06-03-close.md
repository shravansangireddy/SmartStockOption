**Adjustment:** ATH Momentum Entry requires: (1) SPY above prior ATH AND (2) SPY holds gains for first 30 minutes AND (3) VIX declining. Don't enter at open right at resistance.

**Adjustment:** VIX elevation >16 AND SPY failing to make new highs = warning signal. VIX elevation >16 AND SPY still making ATH = not a warning (as seen 6/1-6/2).
