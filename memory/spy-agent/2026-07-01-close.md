## 🧠 Lessons Learned

### 1. **Failed Rally Pattern — The "Midday Trap"**
Today was a textbook failed rally. SPY looked great at midday (+0.30%), the morning thesis was "confirmed" by rising prices, and conviction was raised. But the close told the real story: -0.14%. 

**Lesson:** Never raise conviction at the midday high. Wait for the close. The market's true direction reveals itself in the final hour. **Midday optimism during a rally near resistance is a trap.**

### 2. **QQQ Divergence WAS the Warning — Weight It More Heavily**
At midday, QQQ was already -0.98% while SPY was +0.30%. We flagged this as a "caution" but didn't downgrade conviction enough. By close, QQQ was -1.52%. The QQQ divergence was the most important signal of the day.

**Lesson:** When QQQ underperforms SPY by >1% intraday, **this is a BEARISH signal**, not just a "caution flag." Downgrade conviction immediately.

### 3. **$750 Resistance Is Real**
SPY hit $749 at midday and couldn't break $750. This is the second day where $750 creates hesitation (yesterday SPY stalled at $747.24). The psychological resistance at $750 is proving significant.

**Lesson:** $750 is a validated resistance zone. Don't assume SPY will break through — wait for confirmed volume-backed breakout above $751 before going long near resistance.

### 4. **VIX Bottom-Tick = Market Top-Tick**
VIX dropped to 16.02 at midday (extreme complacency), which coincided exactly with SPY's intraday high. This is a classic pattern — VIX making new lows while the market makes new highs = maximum complacency = reversal risk.

**Lesson:** When VIX drops below 16.5 intraday, treat it as a BEARISH signal for SPY, not bullish. The VIX low = market top pattern is real.

### 5. **Stop at $744 Was Nearly Hit**
SPY closed at $745.76, only $1.76 above the midday tightened stop of $744. If the sell-off had continued for another 30 minutes, the stop would have been hit.

**Lesson:** Tightening stops to the morning open level ($744) was correct risk management. The position was almost stopped out. In future, when QQQ diverges bearishly, consider exiting immediately rather than waiting for the stop.

### 6. **Strategy Signals Remain Absent — 6th Consecutive Day**
Zero signals from any strategy for the 6th straight day. MACD has been in cash for 43+ days. The strategies are not designed for this type of market (near-ATH, low volatility, grinding).

**Lesson:** Daily strategies on SPY are too slow. Either: (a) switch to intraday timeframes for active signals, or (b) accept that systematic strategies will spend long periods in cash near ATH and focus on discretionary analysis.
