## 5. Lessons Learned

### ✅ What Worked
1. **Breakout thesis was correct.** Identified $752.13 as key resistance yesterday; it broke today. The pattern of "multiple days at resistance → eventual breakout" played out perfectly.
2. **Accumulation signal validated.** Yesterday's lesson ("flat day at resistance with no volume spike = accumulation") was correct — the accumulation broke out today.
3. **QQQ leadership confirmed.** We noted tech leading yesterday and this morning; QQQ +0.85% vs SPY +0.56% continued the theme.
4. **VIX complacency was a feature, not a bug.** VIX dropped from 16.46 to 15.61, which supported the rally rather than signaling danger. Low VIX = low expected movement = drift higher.

### ❌ What Didn't Work
1. **Pullback preference cost us 3 days of gains.** For 3 consecutive days, we preferred pullback entries that never triggered. SPY went from ~$749 to $754.70 (+0.76%) while we waited. In a strong trend near ATH, pullbacks are shallow (0.3-0.8%) or nonexistent.
2. **MACD silence is expensive.** MACD exited May 18 at $738.65 and hasn't re-entered. SPY is now $754.70 — that's $16.05 (2.18%) of missed upside. Trend-following systems have long dead zones.
3. **Conservative entry discipline vs. momentum reality.** We keep recommending conservative entries while the market keeps grinding higher. At some point, the opportunity cost exceeds the risk saved.

### 🔄 Key Adjustments
1. **Near ATH, use "shallow pullback" entries only (0.3-0.8%).** Stop waiting for 1.5-2.5% pullbacks that never come. If SPY is within 0.5% of ATH and daily candle is bullish, enter on the next day's open if above prior day's close.
2. **When all timeframes align bullish, reduce entry conservatism.** Full alignment (like today) = accept slightly worse R:R for better timing. Don't wait for the perfect entry in a trending market.
3. **Add "ATH momentum entry" to playbook:** If SPY sets a new 52w high AND closes within 0.2% of it, enter at next day open. Stop loss = 1% below entry. Target = 2-3% continuation.
4. **Track MACD dead zone cost.** Since May 18 exit, MACD has missed +2.18%. Add a "strategy opportunity cost" metric to daily tracking.
5. **VIX < 16 = strong tailwind, not a warning.** Previous threshold of VIX > 18.5 for caution remains, but VIX below 16 should be noted as a bullish accelerant, not just "complacency."
