## 🧠 Lessons Learned

### Lesson #1: The "Wait for Pullback" Bias is Systematically Wrong
**5 consecutive sessions** of preferring a pullback that never came. SPY moved from $749 → $759.08 (+1.3%) while we sat on the sidelines.

- **Root Cause:** Overweighting risk signals (VIX, crypto, gold) and underweighting the actual price action (new highs, full alignment)
- **Fix:** In trending markets near ATH, default to "ATH Momentum Entry" NOT pullback waiting. The pullback preference should only activate when there's an actual reversal signal, not just "concerning" cross-market data.

### Lesson #2: VIX Divergence Was a False Signal (Again)
VIX rose from 15.30 (Friday close) to 16.11 (midday) — up 5.3%. SPY was flat midday, making the divergence look dangerous. **But VIX reversed back to 15.84 by close while SPY rallied to new highs.**

- **Pattern:** VIX intraday spikes in low-vol environments (<17) are noise, not signal. The close matters, not the intraday high.
- **Fix:** Only flag VIX divergence if **VIX closes** above 18.5 AND SPY closes red. Intraday VIX spikes below 18 are meaningless.

### Lesson #3: Late-Day Breakouts on Monday Are a Pattern
SPY was flat all morning/midday, then rallied into the close to set new highs. This is a classic Monday pattern: thin volume early, institutional buying late.

- **Playbook Add:** "Monday Late Breakout" — if SPY is flat to slightly green midday near resistance with no selling pressure, the close is more likely to see a push higher than a reversal.

### Lesson #4: Cross-Market Risk Signals Were Distracting
Morning flagged: VIX up, crypto down, gold up, JPY strengthening. All were true intraday but NONE predicted SPY's direction. Gold reversed, VIX reversed, crypto kept falling but didn't matter for equities.

- **Fix:** Weight cross-market signals at 20%, not 50%. SPY's own price action at key levels should be 80% of the directional call.

### Lesson #5: Conviction Calibration Still Too Low
Morning conviction should have been 7-8/10 given: Friday breakout held, all timeframes aligned, new 52W high imminent. Instead, the "cautiously bullish / no trade" stance had us sitting out a breakout.

- **Fix:** When all 5 timeframes align and price is at/near 52W highs with a recent breakout confirmed, conviction should START at 7/10 minimum.
